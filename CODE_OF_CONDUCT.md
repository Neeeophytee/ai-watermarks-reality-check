# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This project deals with evidence and its limits. The same discipline applies to
how we talk to each other: be accurate, be specific, and do not overstate.

Expected:

- Assume competence. Ask what someone observed before telling them they are wrong.
- Argue about claims and code, not about people.
- Say "I do not know" freely. It is the correct answer here more often than not.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, and any project space.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns by opening a private security advisory (it is the only
private channel this project has) or by contacting a maintainer directly.
Reports will be reviewed and kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this
document, and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations.
